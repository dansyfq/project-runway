# Mode of Financing for a Resale Flat

Plan your finances and work out your budget before you search for a suitable home. Consider the payments that you need to make by using cash and CPF savings, the amount of housing loan, and CPF housing grant(s).

## Cash payments required
- Deposit to seller for Option to Purchase (OTP): you must pay the sellers a deposit ($2 to $5,000), which consists of the Option Fee and Option Exercise Fee, during the granting and exercising of the Option to Purchase respectively.
- Processing fee for Request for Value: if you intend to buy a resale flat using your CPF savings and/or a housing loan, make a Request for Value to HDB to determine the value that will form the basis for your CPF usage and/or the reference for the housing loan amount. The processing fee is $120.
- Resale application fee: an administrative fee of $80 (or $40 for 1- and 2-room flats).
- Legal fees: depend on the solicitor you appoint.
- Part of the initial payment: you must pay part of the initial payment in cash if you are taking a housing loan from an FI or have insufficient CPF savings.
- At resale completion: you must pay the balance purchase price not covered by the initial payment, CPF housing grant(s), CPF Ordinary Account savings and eligible housing loan amount.
- If taking a second HDB housing loan, part of the cash proceeds from disposal of the last flat.
- Others: fire insurance, Home Protection Insurance premium, furnishings, renovation.

## Use of CPF savings
CPF OA savings can be used for legal fees, the initial payment (if taking an FI loan, at least 5% of the initial payment has to be in cash), the balance purchase price, and monthly mortgage instalments after resale completion. Flat buyers taking a housing loan from HDB have the option of retaining up to $20,000 of the available CPF savings in each buyer's OA. The remaining balance must be used to pay for the flat purchase before they can take an HDB housing loan.

## Conditions for use of CPF savings
There is a limit on the total amount of CPF savings that can be used for your flat purchase. Once that limit is reached, you will have to pay the balance flat purchase price and/or monthly mortgage instalments in cash. The total amount depends on the extent the remaining lease of the flat can cover the youngest buyer up to the age of 95. You can get the details of a flat's lease at HDB Map.
- Remaining lease is at least 20 years and can cover the youngest buyer up to the age of 95: the amount used is capped at the lower of the value of flat or price of the flat at the time of purchase.
- It cannot cover the youngest buyer to 95: the amount is pro-rated based on the extent the remaining lease of the flat can cover the youngest buyer up to the age of 95. When the allowed CPF amount is used up, buyers have to pay the balance flat purchase price and/or monthly mortgage instalments in cash. This will help buyers to set aside CPF savings for their housing needs during retirement (e.g. buying a replacement property).

## Housing loan from HDB
You need a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter before obtaining an OTP from a flat seller and when you submit your resale application. Core members must physically occupy the flat during the minimum occupation period (MOP). You and all core members must not have taken 2 or more housing loans from HDB. You and your co-applicant(s) may each retain up to $20,000 CPF savings in the OA. If the lease can cover the youngest core member up to the age of 95 and above, the LTV limit will be 75% of the lower of the resale price and value. Otherwise, the LTV limit will be pro-rated from 75%. You may refinance an HDB housing loan with a loan from an FI.

## Housing loan from financial institutions
You must have a valid Letter of Offer before exercising the OTP. An FI loan can be refinanced with the same or different FI, but not with HDB.

## CPF housing grants
You may be eligible for CPF housing grants, which are housing subsidies given by the Government to eligible Singapore Citizen buyers. The grants can be used for the initial payment and to reduce the required housing loan amount.
