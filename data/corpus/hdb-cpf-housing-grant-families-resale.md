# CPF Housing Grants for Families Buying Resale Flats

Under the CPF Housing Grant Scheme, you may qualify for a housing subsidy of up to $80,000 to help with the purchase of a resale flat. In addition, you may also receive the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Families) and the Proximity Housing Grant (Families) if you meet the respective eligibility conditions.

A household's eligibility for housing subsidies and HDB housing loan is assessed based on the core family nucleus. All core members must remain in the flat application, and physically reside in the flat during the minimum occupation period (MOP) after the flat purchase.

## Eligibility conditions (Family Grant)
- Assistance for: couples, families, or orphaned siblings who are first-timer applicants buying an HDB resale flat. If you are buying over your child's flat, you will not be eligible for housing grants.
- Citizenship: you must be a Singapore Citizen (SC) and include at least 1 other SC or Singapore Permanent Resident (SPR).
- Age: at least 21 years old.
- Household status: at least 1 core member is a first-timer to receive the housing grant. First-timer core applicants and/or occupiers must not have taken any housing subsidy before.
- Monthly household income ceiling: average gross monthly household income must not exceed $16,000, or $24,000 if purchasing with an extended or multi-generation family.
- Private property: applicants and occupiers must not own or have an interest in any local or overseas private residential property, and must not have disposed of one in the last 30 months before the HFE letter application.
- Flat type: 2-room or bigger.
- Remaining lease of flat: more than 20 years. You can get the details of a flat lease at HDB Map.

## Amount
First-timer households buying a 2- to 4-room resale flat: SC/SC $80,000; SC/SPR $70,000.
First-timer households buying a 5-room or bigger resale flat: SC/SC $50,000; SC/SPR $40,000.
First-timer and second-timer couples: 2- to 4-room resale flat $40,000; 5-room or bigger $25,000.

The grant is credited into the first-timer SC and SPR core members' CPF Ordinary Accounts and can be used to offset the purchase price of the flat or reduce the housing loan required. Occupiers cannot use their CPF savings (including housing grants) for the flat purchase.
