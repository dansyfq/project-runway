# Conditions After Buying a Resale Flat

The conditions apply to both flat owners and occupiers. Core members must remain in the flat application and physically reside in the flat during the minimum occupation period (MOP). They cannot apply for or be listed in an HFE letter to buy an HDB flat, or buy an Executive Condominium unit from a developer, during the MOP.

## Taking a housing subsidy
Members of the core nucleus have taken a housing subsidy if they have bought a flat from HDB, a resale flat with CPF Housing Grant, a DBSS flat from a developer, or an EC unit from a developer. An SC/SPR household may apply for a Citizen Top-Up of $10,000 when the SPR family member obtains citizenship or when they have an SC child.

## Disposal of existing HDB flat or private residential property
You or any person listed in the flat must dispose of any interest in any HDB flat and/or private residential property within 6 months from resale completion of the flat purchase (based on the legal completion date of the disposal). Anyone renting an HDB flat must surrender it within 4 months from legal completion.

## Minimum occupation period (MOP)
All persons listed in an unclassified/Standard flat must physically reside in the flat for a minimum period of 5 years before they may apply for an HFE letter, sell the flat on the open market, rent out the whole flat, or acquire interest in private property (local or overseas). The MOP starts from the legal completion date and excludes any period where you do not physically reside in the flat. For new and resale Plus and Prime flats, the MOP is 10 years. For flats purchased under the Fresh Start Housing Scheme, the MOP is 20 years.

## Resale levy
The resale levy reduces the housing subsidy of a second subsidised flat. If you have bought subsidised housing, you must pay a resale levy when buying a second subsidised flat. If you are buying a resale flat or private property, you need not pay the resale levy. Payment must be made using cash and/or flat sale proceeds, not a housing loan.
For a first subsidised flat sold on or after 3 March 2006, fixed resale levy (Family / Single): 2-room or 2-room Flexi $15,000 / $7,500; 3-room $30,000 / $15,000; 4-room $40,000 / $20,000; 5-room $45,000 / $22,500; 3Gen $45,000 / not applicable; Executive $50,000 / $25,000; Executive Condominium $55,000 / $27,500.
