# Proximity Housing Grant for Families Buying Resale Flats

If you are buying a resale flat to live with or near your parents/child, you may apply for the Proximity Housing Grant (PHG): $30,000 to live with your parents/child; $20,000 to live near your parents/child (within 4km). You may also receive the CPF Housing Grant for Resale Flats (Families), Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (Families) and Step-Up CPF Housing Grant (Families) if you meet the eligibility conditions.

## Eligibility conditions
- Assistance for: couples or families who have not taken the PHG previously. Parents buying over their child's flat are not eligible.
- Citizenship: you must be a Singapore Citizen and include at least 1 other SC or SPR.
- Age: at least 21 years old.
- Proximity condition: your parents/child will live with you in the resale flat (they must be included in the HFE letter and resale application and live there during the MOP), or your parents/child live in an HDB flat or private residential property within 4 km of the resale flat you intend to buy, and must observe the proximity rule during the MOP.
- Remaining lease of flat: more than 20 years.

The PHG is credited into CPF Ordinary Accounts of SC and SPR core members and can be used to offset the purchase price or reduce the housing loan required.
