# Request for Value for a Resale Flat

After the sellers have granted you an Option to Purchase (OTP), the Option Period will commence from the Option Date. The Option Period is 21 calendar days (including Saturdays, Sundays, and Public Holidays). During this period, you must log in to My Flat Dashboard to confirm your mode of financing and make a Request for Value; after the outcome is available, confirm your decision whether to buy the flat.

The Request for Value will determine the value that will form the basis for your CPF usage and/or the reference for the housing loan amount from HDB or the financial institution (FI), unless otherwise advised by the FI. If you are using cash only (no CPF usage and no housing loan), you do not need to submit a Request for Value.

You must submit the Request for Value via My Flat Dashboard by the next working day after the Option Date, together with a scanned copy of Page 1 of the OTP, and pay a processing fee of $120 (including GST). If a valuation is required, the appointed valuer will contact the flat seller to arrange an inspection. The outcome is typically available within 10 working days.

The outcome of the Request for Value is valid for 3 months. You and the flat sellers must submit the respective portions of the resale application during this period. You have to wait for the outcome of the Request for Value before you may exercise the OTP during the Option Period.
