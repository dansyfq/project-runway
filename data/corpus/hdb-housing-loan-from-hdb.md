# Housing Loan from HDB

If you need financing to buy or take over the ownership of a flat, you may apply for a housing loan from HDB or the financial institutions (FI). Apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter via the HDB Flat Portal for a holistic understanding of your housing and financing options before you embark on your home buying journey. It will inform you upfront of your eligibility to buy a new or resale flat, as well as the amount of housing grants and HDB housing loan you are eligible for.

## Eligibility conditions
- Citizenship: at least 1 applicant is a Singapore Citizen.
- Household status: all the members of the core family nucleus in your HFE letter application have not taken 2 or more housing loans from HDB.
- Intended flat purchase: Singapore citizens aged 55 and above are not applying for a 2-room Flexi flat on short lease. Singles aged 35 and above are buying a 2-room Flexi flat on 99-year lease from HDB, or a 5-room or smaller resale flat. Housing loan is not available for 2-room Flexi flats on short lease or Community Care Apartments; you must pay using cash and/or CPF Ordinary Account savings.
- Monthly household income ceiling: average gross monthly household income must not exceed $16,000 for families; $24,000 for extended families; $8,000 for singles buying under the Single Singapore Citizen (SSC) Scheme.
- Private residential property: all applicants and occupiers must not own or have an interest in any local or overseas private residential property, and must not have disposed of any private residential property in the last 30 months (from the legal completion date) before the HFE letter application.
- Non-residential property: the household can own or have an interest in up to 1 non-residential property at HFE letter application.

## Remaining lease of the flat
The loan amount will depend on the extent the remaining lease can cover the youngest applicant to the age of 95 and above.

Remaining lease of flat is more than 20 years and covers youngest applicant to age of:
- 95 years and above: Loan-to-Value (LTV) limit for a new flat is up to 75% of the purchase price; for a resale flat, up to 75% of the lower of the resale price or value of the flat.
- Less than 95 years: a lower loan limit pro-rated from 75%, based on the extent the remaining lease can cover the youngest applicant to the age of 95 and above.
- Loan period: shortest of 25 years; 65 years minus the average age of the applicants; and remaining lease at the point of flat application minus 20 years.

The LTV limit refers to the maximum amount of housing loan a flat applicant may take up, expressed as a percentage of the lower of the flat price or value of the flat. The 75% LTV applies to complete resale applications received by HDB on or after 20 August 2024 and flat applications for the October 2024 sales exercise onwards. Applicants who have submitted a resale application will be provided with a customised financial plan, which will include the applicable LTV limit at the point of flat application.

## Conditions when taking a second HDB housing loan
Your second HDB housing loan amount will be granted only after you have paid for the flat purchase using the full CPF refund and part of the cash proceeds received from the disposal of your existing or last-owned HDB flat (or last-owned private residential property disposed of at least 30 months before your HFE letter application). You and all members of the core family nucleus must use the CPF refund and up to 50% of the cash proceeds. You may retain up to $20,000 in your CPF Ordinary Account. If you buy an HDB flat before disposing of the existing one, you will be charged commercial interest rate until you have disposed of the existing flat within 6 months from legal completion and used the CPF refund and up to 50% of cash proceeds to redeem the loan. Generally, flat applicants can keep the higher of $25,000 or 50% of the cash proceeds.

If you do not meet the eligibility conditions, you have to take a housing loan from an FI if you need mortgage financing.

## Details on the HDB housing loan
The loan amount is determined by the flat applicants' financial situation: age, monthly income, job stability, current loans and financial commitments, past repayment records and monthly cash savings. The applicant must be working at the point of HFE letter application and when HDB disburses the housing loan.
- Repayment period: capped at whichever is the shortest of 25 years; 65 years minus the average age of the applicants; or remaining lease of the flat minus 20 years.
- Applicable interest rate: HDB uses the higher of the interest rate floor (currently 3.0% per annum) and the prevailing HDB housing loan interest rate to compute the eligible housing loan amount. The concessionary interest rate is pegged at 0.1%-point above the prevailing CPF Ordinary Account interest rate and reviewed quarterly.
- Monthly instalments: up to 30% of the applicants' monthly income.
- LTV limit: up to 75% of the lower of the resale price or value of the flat for resale flats. If the remaining lease of the flat does not cover the youngest applicant to the age of 95 and above at the point of flat application, the LTV limit will be pro-rated from 75%.

HDB housing loan is only disbursed at key collection. Once disbursed, you will not be able to take up another HDB housing loan for 30 months from the legal completion of the flat purchase.

## Use of CPF savings
Applicants have the option to retain up to $20,000 of the available savings in each of their CPF Ordinary Account (OA), and use OA savings to pay the stamp fee, registration fee, legal fees, and premium for the CPF Home Protection Insurance. The remaining balance in your CPF OA must be used to pay for the flat purchase before the HDB housing loan can be granted.

The total amount of CPF savings that can be used to buy the flat and pay the monthly mortgage instalments will depend on the extent that the remaining lease of the flat can cover the youngest applicant up to the age of 95, and applicable CPF usage limits. When the allowed CPF amount is used up, you have to pay for the balance purchase price and/or the monthly mortgage instalments in cash. Occupiers cannot use their CPF OA savings or housing grant for the flat purchase or servicing of housing loan.

## Insurance for HDB housing loan
If you take an HDB housing loan you must buy the HDB Fire Insurance Scheme, and the Home Protection Scheme (HPS) if you use CPF savings to pay monthly loan instalments. HPS is a mortgage-reducing insurance scheme administered by CPF Board that insures members against losing their home should the policy holder become permanently incapacitated or pass away before the housing loan is paid up.
