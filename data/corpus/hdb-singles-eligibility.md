# Flat, Grant and Loan Eligibility: Singles

These conditions apply to a Singapore citizen who is unmarried, widowed or divorced, aged 35 and above, and buying a flat on their own. Singles may apply for a 2-room Flexi flat from HDB or buy a resale flat on the open market as a core applicant.

## Eligibility conditions for resale flats
- Flat type: resale unclassified, Standard and Plus flats - all flat types (excluding 3Gen flats) in any location; up to 5-room if applying for CPF housing grants.
- Citizenship: you must be a Singapore Citizen.
- Age: at least 35 years old (unmarried or divorced); at least 21 years old (widowed or an orphan).
- Monthly household income ceiling: unclassified/Standard flat - no income ceiling to buy; Plus flat - $16,000; 2-room Flexi (99-year) and 2-room resale Prime flats - $8,000. The income ceiling applies to qualify for CPF housing grants (excluding Proximity Housing Grant) and HDB housing loan.
- Private residential property: if buying with CPF housing grants or an HDB housing loan, you must not own or have an interest in any private residential property and must not have disposed of one within 30 months before the HFE letter application. If buying a non-subsidised resale unclassified/Standard flat, you must dispose of any private residential property within 6 months of completing your flat purchase.
