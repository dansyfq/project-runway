# Application for an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter

Log in to the HDB Flat Portal using your Singpass to apply for an HFE letter. The HFE letter provides a holistic understanding of your housing and financing options and helps you plan your budget before you embark on your home buying journey. Second-timers will also be informed of the resale levy/premium payable for the purchase of a subsidised flat from HDB.

You may request an In-Principle Approval (IPA) from participating financial institutions when you apply for an HFE letter. The IPA gives the FI's indicative loan amount. The service is free of charge.

## When to apply
If you are buying a resale flat, you must have a valid HFE letter before you obtain an Option to Purchase (OTP) from a flat seller, and when you submit your resale application to HDB. The processing time is up to a month after HDB has received the full set of required documents, and may be longer around sales exercises. Apply early.

You may only apply online and the application is free. You may only be listed in 1 HFE letter application, either as an applicant or occupier. Your flat application will be processed based on the HFE letter you submitted.

## How to apply
Step 1: Check Preliminary HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE). Step 2: Apply for HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter. Complete both steps within 30 calendar days of each other. All applicants and required occupiers need a valid Singpass account. The applicants and occupiers in your flat application must remain the same as those in your HFE letter application.

## Validity
The HFE letter is valid for 9 months from the date of issue. The outcome may be reviewed if any information is untrue, if the household does not comply with the terms and conditions to buy a flat, apply for CPF housing grants or take an HDB housing loan, or if there are changes in household circumstances.
