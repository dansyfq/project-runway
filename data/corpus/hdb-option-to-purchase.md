# Option to Purchase When Buying a Resale Flat

After you have obtained a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter, considered your mode of financing and worked out your budget, you may look for a suitable flat. Once you have decided on a flat and agreed on a price, you may obtain an Option to Purchase (OTP) from the flat sellers.

If you intend to get an HDB housing loan, you must have a valid HFE letter indicating your eligibility for HDB housing loan before the flat sellers may grant you an OTP. If you intend to get a housing loan from a financial institution, you must have a valid Letter of Offer (LO) before you exercise the OTP.

You and the flat sellers must use the HDB-prescribed OTP. Any other or supplementary agreements relating to the sale or purchase are not valid under the Housing and Development Act. The OTP is a legally binding contract.

## Deposit to sellers
- Option Fee, paid when the sellers grant the OTP: between $1 and $1,000.
- Option Exercise Fee, paid when you exercise the OTP: an amount which, including the Option Fee, will not exceed $5,000.
These amounts are negotiable and form part of the resale price.

## Steps
1. Negotiate and agree on the resale price. Check recent transacted resale flat prices. You and the sellers must declare the true resale price to HDB.
2. Sellers grant the OTP. The option expires 21 calendar days from the option date, including weekends and public holidays (e.g. granted on 1 April, it expires on 22 April at 4pm). Once granted, the sellers cannot grant another OTP until yours expires.
3. Review the purchase during the 21-day Option Period: confirm your mode of financing (cash, CPF savings and/or housing loan) in My Flat Dashboard and submit a Request for Value if you are using CPF savings or a housing loan. Use the Option Period wisely and do not rush into exercising the OTP.
