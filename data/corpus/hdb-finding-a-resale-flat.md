# Finding a Resale Flat

Apply for an HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter via My Flat Dashboard for a holistic understanding of your housing and financing options before you embark on your home buying journey. You can concurrently apply for an In-principle Approval letter for a housing loan from participating financial institutions. The HFE letter tells you upfront your eligibility to buy a new or resale flat, and the amounts of CPF housing grants and HDB housing loan you are eligible for.

## Resale Flat Listing (RFL) service
- Shortlist flats: use filters such as location, flat type, flat price, remaining lease, and your ethnicity and citizenship.
- View flat details: photos, listing price and descriptions, flat type, floor area, original floor plan, remaining lease, EIP and SPR quota for the current month, and recent transacted prices of nearby flats.
- Compare flats: favourite shortlisted flats and compare attributes and preliminary payment plans.
- Arrange flat viewing appointments: if you have a valid HFE letter, you may contact flat sellers or their salespersons.

## Other resources
- Resale seminars: learn about resale policies and procedures.
- Resale statistics and resale flat prices: check recent transacted prices in the location you are interested in. These prices reflect market conditions at the time of the transactions; you may refer to them to set your offer price.
- HDB Map: obtain details such as the commencement dates of flat leases and resale prices.
- Distance enquiry for Proximity Housing Grant: check whether the flat is within 4 km of your parent's or child's residence.

You must have a valid HFE letter when you obtain an Option to Purchase (OTP) from a flat seller, and when both you and the seller submit the resale application to HDB.
