# Ethnic Integration Policy (EIP) and Singapore Permanent Resident Quota

When buying an HDB resale flat, you must ensure your household is within the EIP quota for the block and neighbourhood, and if applicable, the SPR quota as well. Non-Malaysian SPR households must meet the SPR quota.

You may be eligible to buy a flat if you and the flat seller are of the same ethnic group and household citizenship; or after your flat purchase, the number of households of your ethnic group remains within the block and neighbourhood limits (and, for non-Malaysian SPR households, the number of SPR households remains within the SPR quota).

The prevailing EIP and SPR quotas are displayed on the resale flat listings on the HDB Flat Portal and updated on the 1st of each month. Check compliance when the sellers grant the OTP, when you exercise the OTP, and when you and the sellers submit the resale application.

The EIP preserves Singapore's multi-cultural identity and promotes racial integration and harmony by ensuring a balanced mix of ethnic communities in HDB towns. Non-Malaysian SPR households need to be within the SPR quota for the block (8%) and neighbourhood (5%).
